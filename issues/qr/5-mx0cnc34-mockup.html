Static vs dynamic: which do you need?

Static codes

The data is printed in the pattern itself.

  • Never expire, work offline for text and Wi-Fi
  • Right for menus, Wi-Fi cards, business cards, packaging, signs
  • Cannot change the destination after printing

Dynamic codes

The pattern holds a short redirect link.

  • Work only while the provider keeps redirecting
  • Right for campaigns where you must change the target or track scans
  • Check the real yearly price and what happens if you stop paying
