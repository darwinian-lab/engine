Questions

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Open the camera app, point it at the code and tap the banner to add the event to your calendar.

Why did my scanner app show plain text?

Some third-party scanner apps do not understand calendar data. Use the phone's built-in camera instead.

Can I change the date after printing?

No. A static code cannot change, so check the date and time before you print.
