What to check before you tap

  1. Read the address. The part just before the first single slash is who you are really visiting.
  2. Look for a sticker. Peel at the corner: a fake is often stuck over the real code.
  3. Distrust urgency. “Pay now or be fined” is a scam signal.
  4. Never enter a password or card after a code you did not expect.
  5. When unsure, type the site yourself or use the official app.
