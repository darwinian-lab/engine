QRFY: do my codes stop working when the free trial ends?

Short answer

Dynamic codes made during QRFY's 7-day free trial stop working when the trial ends, according to QRFY's own support page. Static codes do not depend on QRFY.

Not sure which kind you have? Scan it with your camera: if the address starts with qrfy.com or another short link, it is dynamic.

✓ Last verified 28 Nov 2026 against QRFY's support page

What QRFY says

“Dynamic QR Codes created during the 7-day free trial period will stop working (deactivate) once the trial ends.”QRFY support, quoted as published. Checked 28 Nov 2026.

Source: QRFY support page (vendor's own wording). We only list what a vendor publishes. If their page changes, this page is updated and re-dated.

Not covered by QRFY's page: what happens after you pay and later cancel. We say nothing about it until it is published.

Is my code static or dynamic?

Dynamic

The code holds a short link on the vendor's domain. The vendor forwards scans to your page, so it can switch off.

Static

The code holds your real address or text. No vendor sits in between, so nothing can be deactivated.
