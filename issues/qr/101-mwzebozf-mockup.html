How to use a WiFi QR code, honestly

EncodeNetwork name, password, security type
Print size3 × 3 cm minimum; 5 × 5 cm on a wall
Expires?No. It is static. Change the password and you need a new code.

What to encode

The exact network name and password, with matching capitals. Pick the security type from your router's label; WPA covers most home and cafe routers.

Where people place it

On the counter, in a frame by the till, in guest-room welcome cards, on the fridge at home. Keep it at eye or hand height.

Size to print

At least 3 × 3 cm for a table card, bigger for anything scanned from further than an arm's length. Leave a blank border around it.

Common mistakes

Typing the password with a wrong capital, choosing the wrong security type, or printing before testing on both an iPhone and an Android.

Using your main WiFi for guests? Consider a separate guest network, since anyone who scans the code gets the password.
