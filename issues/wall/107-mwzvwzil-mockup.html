Where testimonials work best

  • Near the call-to-action: put proof right beside the button.
  • On the pricing page: it answers “is this worth it?” at the moment of doubt.
  • On the landing page: a wall below the first screen builds trust fast.
  • What to show: name, role, a photo if given, and a specific result.
