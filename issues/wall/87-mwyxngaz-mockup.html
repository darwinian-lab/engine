What we add to your wall page

Only approved, public testimonials are counted. The numbers are calculated from the reviews on the page, never typed in by hand.

<script type="application/ld+json">
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "LocalBusiness",
  "name": "Lena Flow Yoga",
  "aggregateRating": {
    "@type": "AggregateRating",
    "ratingValue": "4.9",

  "review": [{

Honest by design

Google limits "self-serving" reviews, meaning ones a business collects about itself. We follow that, so you don't get penalised.

What we do

  • Count only approved reviews that are visible on the page
  • Mark up real, individual reviews with the author's name
  • Put the markup on the page that shows those reviews
  • Leave markup out when there are no public reviews

What we won't do

  • Invent or inflate ratings or review counts
  • Add markup for reviews hidden from visitors
  • Promise stars in search results
  • Charge extra for it
Heads up: Google may not show stars for local-business or organisation reviews you collected yourself. The markup still helps Google understand your page, and you can switch it off per wall.
