Testimonial request templates for personal trainers

Four messages you can copy, tweak and send today: email, WhatsApp, a face-to-face script and a gentle reminder.

  • Email
  • WhatsApp / SMS
  • In person
  • Reminder

When to ask

  • Right after a milestone: first pull-up, a 10-week block done, a race finished.
  • At a progress check-in, when the client is proud.
  • Never mid-plateau. Wait for a win.

What makes one convincing

  • A specific outcome: 'lost 9 kg in 12 weeks', not 'great trainer'.
  • The before: what they struggled with first.
  • Permission: a clear yes to show their first name and words publicly.
Email

After a milestone

Subject: Could I quote you on your 12 weeks? Hi [Name], Congratulations on [milestone]. Seeing you go from [where they started] to [result] has been the highlight of my month. Would you write 2-3 sentences about what changed for you? What was hard before, and what is different now? Here is the link, it takes two minutes and needs no account: [your Kudospage link] I will only show your first name and your words, and only if you tick the box that says yes. Thank you, [Your name]

Tip: name the milestone. It reminds them of the result they should write about.

WhatsApp / SMS

Quick message

Hi [Name]! So proud of your [milestone] 💪 Would you leave me a couple of lines on how training has gone for you? Just tap here: [your Kudospage link]. A number or two (kg, weeks, how you feel) helps a lot. Thank you!

Send mid-morning or early evening, not before a session.

In person

Script at the end of a session

'That was your best session yet. Can I ask a favour? New clients keep asking what it is really like. Would you write two lines about where you started and where you are now? I will text you a link so you can do it on your phone before you leave the gym.'

Show the link on your phone and let them write while it is fresh.

Follow-up

Gentle reminder after 5 days

Hi [Name], no pressure at all, but if you have two minutes this week I would love your words about our training: [your Kudospage link]. If it is not for you, that is completely fine. Thanks either way!

Remind once, then stop. One polite nudge roughly doubles replies without annoying anyone.

Now collect them in one place

Send one link to every client, approve the replies you like, and show them as a wall on your site. Free, no card.
