Frequently asked questions

Is Kudospage free?

Yes. The free plan has no time limit and needs no credit card.

Do customers need an account to leave a testimonial?

No. They open a link, pick a star rating and write a few words.

Where can I show the wall?

Anywhere you can paste one line of code: WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, Wix, Squarespace, Carrd or plain HTML.

Can I get my testimonials out?

Yes. Export them whenever you like.
