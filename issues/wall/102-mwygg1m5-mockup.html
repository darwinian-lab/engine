When to ask a photography client

Ask while the memory is warm and the invoice is settled. Call it feedback, not a testimonial: people write more naturally.

Best

Two days after gallery delivery

Clients have just seen the photos and are still excited.

Good

After they print or share

Their family's reaction makes a vivid quote.

Hold off

Open invoice or late delivery

Wait until it is paid and the gallery has arrived.

Five prompts tuned to photography

  1. 1How did you feel in front of the camera before the shoot, and after?
  2. 2What was your favourite moment or photo from the gallery?
  3. 3How was working with me, from booking to delivery?
  4. 4What would you tell a friend who is nervous about booking a session?
  5. 5Anything we could do even better next time?

Photography cautions

  • Ask permission before showing a client's name or a photo of their children.
  • Only publish words the client wrote. Never edit a quote to say more.
  • Do not offer a discount or free print in exchange for a good review.
