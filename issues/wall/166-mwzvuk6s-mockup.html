Can I use a customer's text, email or LinkedIn recommendation as a testimonial?

Yes, if you have their clear permission, ideally in writing.

Tell them where it will appear: which quote, on which website, and whether their name or photo is shown.

Ask with this message

Works for text and email. Replace the highlighted parts.

Hi Sam, thank you for your kind message on 12 March: “The new site brought in three bookings in a week.” May I show it as a testimonial on lenaflowyoga.com? I would show it with your first name and photo (or: without your name). Reply “yes” to allow it, or tell me what you would change. You can ask me to remove it at any time.

Before you publish

  • Keep a recordSave the reply, with its date, for as long as the quote is live.
  • Don't edit the meaningTrim for length if they agree, but never change what they said.
  • Say if they were rewardedA discount, gift or payment must be made clear.
  • Check the rules for regulated fieldsHealth, finance and legal services often have stricter advertising rules.
