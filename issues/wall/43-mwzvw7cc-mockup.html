1. The short template (3 questions)

Short and easy to answer

Works for most work
Subject: Three quick questions about working together

Hi [name],

Thanks again for [working with me / choosing us]. Would you answer three quick questions? A sentence or two each is plenty.

1. What was the situation before we started?
2. What changed after?
3. Who would you recommend [me / us] to?

With your OK, I'd like to share your answers on my website. I'll check with you before anything goes live.

Thank you,
[your name]

2. Five more copy-ready templates

After a project

Subject: How did the [project] turn out for you?

Hi [name], now that [project] is wrapped up, I'd love to hear how it went. What was the biggest difference it made for [company]? A couple of sentences is perfect, and I'll only share it with your approval.

Thanks, [your name]

After a purchase

Subject: How is your [product] working out?

Hi [name], you've had your [product] for a few days now. What made you choose it, and how is it going? Your honest answer helps the next buyer decide.

[your name]

For a coach or course student

Subject: What changed for you since [course / program]?

Hi [name], it was great to watch your progress. Where were you when you started, and where are you now? If one moment stood out, tell me about it.

Cheering you on, [your name]

For a SaaS user

Subject: Quick question about [product]

Hi [name], I noticed you've been using [feature] for [time]. What problem were you trying to solve, and has [product] helped? One or two sentences is plenty.

Thanks, [your name]

Polite reminder

Send after 5 days
Subject: Re: Three quick questions

Hi [name], a gentle nudge in case this got buried. Even one sentence helps. If now isn't a good time, no worries at all.

Thanks, [your name]

3. A video testimonial request

Would you record a 30-second phone video saying what you were struggling with and what's better now? Don't script it, just talk to the camera and send me the file or link.

4. When to ask and when to follow up

  • Ask within about a week of finishing the work, while the result is fresh.
  • Ask at a high point: after good news, a delivered result or a thank-you from the client.
  • Follow up once after 5 days with the reminder above, then let it go.
  • Make it one click: a link they can answer in under two minutes.

Response rates vary by business and relationship, and no number is guaranteed.

5. How to get permission to publish a name, role and photo

Ask in the same message, and say exactly what you will show. Let people choose how much appears.

Sample line: “May I publish your answers with your first name, role and company, and your photo if you'd like to send one? I'll show you the final version first.”

6. Common mistakes

Asking too lateMonths later the details are gone and the reply is vague.
A blank-page request“Write us a testimonial” is hard. Specific questions are easy.
Editing the wording too heavilyFix typos only. Heavily polished quotes stop sounding like a real customer.

8. FAQ

How do I ask a customer for a testimonial?

Send a short, personal message with three specific questions within about a week of finishing the work.

How long should the email be?

Under 100 words. Short requests are easier to answer.

What if they don't reply?

Send one polite reminder after 5 days, then leave it.

Do I need permission to publish a testimonial?

Yes. Ask for permission to use their name, role and photo, and show them the final wording.
