Military to civilian resume

Short answer: swap rank and unit jargon for plain job words. Say what you led, how many people, and what the result was. Employers should not need a glossary.

BeforeNCOIC of S-3 shop, supervised 12 soldiers, ran TTP training.
AfterSupervised a team of 12, planned and delivered training that cut onboarding time by a third.
