Switching from another resume builder

Can I move my resume out of Zety without paying? Yes.Open your resume's free preview, copy the text or save the PDF, and paste it here. It is read in this browser and nothing is uploaded. Works for Zety, Resume.io, Resume Genius, Canva, Enhancv and Novoresume.

1. Get your text out

From Zety, no payment needed

  1. Open your resume and choose Preview, not Download.
  2. Select all the text on the preview (Ctrl/Cmd + A) and copy it.
  3. Paste it below. A saved PDF of the preview also works.

Watch for: skill bars drop out, and two-column layouts can come out in the wrong order. We handle both.
