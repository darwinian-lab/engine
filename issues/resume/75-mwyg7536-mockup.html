Turn your resume bullets into interview stories

A good bullet and a good interview answer are the same story at different lengths. Pick 3 to 5 bullets and jot down the outline.

🔒A notes outline, not a script. Short prompts to jog your memory, so you sound like yourself. Nothing is uploaded and no AI is used. Your bullets are read from this browser only.

1. Pick your stories 3 of 5

From the Experience on your resume. Choose ones you can talk about for a minute.

3. Which story answers which question?

One story can answer several questions. Aim to cover each one.

Tell me about a time you improved a process.
Tell me about a time you helped a teammate learn something.
Describe a problem you solved with limited resources.
Tell me about a time you were proud of your results.
Tell me about a time you handled a mistake or a setback.
