7 tips for a barista resume

  1. Lead with your speed and your people skillsPut a two-line summary first: years of experience, the type of venue and one strength.
  2. Show volumeHiring managers want to know you can handle a rush.
    EXAMPLEServed about 250 customers per weekday shift.
  3. Name the equipmentMention the machines and POS systems you've used, such as La Marzocco or Square.
  4. Count what you canWaste cut, add-on sales, drinks per hour. Use your real figures only; ours are invented.
  5. List certificatesFood hygiene and allergen training can go right under the summary.
  6. Include availabilityEarly starts and weekends help. Say so in one line.
  7. No experience? Use school, volunteering or family workSee the student / first job example.
