6 hints found: low signalProbably not worth a full rewrite of your resume.

These are hints, not proof. We can't know whether a job is real, and plenty of good jobs have messy ads.

  • No pay range

    The ad doesn't mention salary or hourly pay.

    Ask about pay at the first call.

  • No location or remote policy

    You can't tell where the work happens.

    Check this before you apply, or ask in your first message.

  • Long must-have list for a junior role

    12 skills are listed for a junior title.

    Apply, but don't spend an hour tailoring.

  • Wording: rockstar, fast-paced family

    This can point to long hours or blurred boundaries, or it may just be clumsy writing.

    Ask what a normal week looks like.

  • Unrealistic experience: 8+ years

    That doesn't fit a junior title.

    Apply if you meet the core of it. Don't be put off.

  • Talent pool wording and no named team or contact

    Ads like this are sometimes collecting CVs for later.

    Keep it short, and don't expect a quick reply.
