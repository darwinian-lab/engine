Pricing / When Pro ends

If I stop paying, what happens to my resume?

  • Every resume, version and cover letter stays. Nothing is deleted when Pro ends.
  • The PDF always downloads. It was free before Pro and stays free.
  • Only new Pro actions pause, such as Word export and saving new versions.
