How to spot a 'free' builder that charges at download

Check these before you type in your details.

  • Does the PDF button work before you enter anything?Try it on the empty page. If it asks you to pay or sign up only after you've done the work, that's the catch.
  • Is a card requested for a 'trial'?A free trial that needs a card is usually a subscription waiting to start.
  • Does the price renew?Look for 'then per month' in small print, and whether you can cancel without emailing anyone.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Building the resume and downloading the PDF cost nothing, and there is no watermark.

Do I need to register?

No. There is no account and no email address to give.

Is the PDF ATS-friendly?

Every template is one column with real text and standard headings, which applicant tracking software generally reads well. We haven't tested with any particular ATS product. To check yours, copy all the text from the PDF and paste it into a plain text editor.

Can I edit later?

Yes. Come back in the same browser and your resume is still there. Change anything and download a new PDF.

What if I clear my browser?

Your resume is stored only in your browser, so clearing site data deletes it. Download a PDF first as a backup.

Is there any plan to charge?

Not now. If we ever add extras, they would be one clear one-time price, never a subscription. Downloading your resume stays free.
